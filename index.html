BaraTek, by Joy Anne

Founder & Builder

I built BaraTek because I believe businesses should not have to make important decisions in the dark.

Data is everywhere. In sales, customers, operations, finance and logistics. But raw data only becomes valuable when we know how to understand it.

BaraTek is my attempt to build that bridge: between messy information and clear decisions, using technology to help businesses understand what their data is telling them.
